# PART 5 — MIS & DATA

## 19. What is MIS?

**MIS = Management Information System.**

Good interview answer:

> “MIS is a system or process used to collect, organize and present information in a useful form for management. For example, a school could use MIS reports to understand admissions, student records, fees or other operational information and use that information for decision-making.”

---

## 20. What is the difference between data and information?

> “Data is the raw collection of facts, while information is processed and organized data that has meaning and can support a decision.”

Example:

**Data:** 500 individual student records.

**Information:** “There are 500 students, with 120 in Grade 8.”

---

## 21. What is a report?

> “A report organizes relevant information so that a person can understand a situation, monitor performance or take action.”

---

## 22. What makes a good report?

> “A good report should be accurate, relevant, clear, easy to understand and delivered at the right time. It should focus on information that helps the person receiving it make a decision or take action.”

# PART 8 — WORKFLOW

## 29. What is a workflow?

> “A workflow is the sequence of steps through which a task or process moves from beginning to completion, including the people involved, information required, approvals and systems used.”

Example:

**Admission enquiry → application → verification → approval → enrollment**

---

## 30. What is process mapping?

> “Process mapping means visually or logically representing the steps in a process, including who performs each step, what information is required and what the output is.”

---

## 31. What is an SOP?

**SOP = Standard Operating Procedure.**

> “An SOP is a documented set of instructions explaining how a recurring task or process should be performed consistently.”

---

## 32. What is a bottleneck?

> “A bottleneck is a step in a process that slows down the overall process because work gets delayed or accumulated at that point.”

---

## 33. How would you improve a process?

Use this answer structure:

> “First I would understand the existing process. Then I would identify where delays, duplication or errors occur. I would discuss the issue with the people involved, suggest possible improvements, implement an appropriate solution and then check whether the process actually improved.”
